# My Obsidian Workflow

Notes on my current Obsidian setup. Still evolving.

## Folder Structure

I use a modified PARA/Zettelkasten hybrid:

- **00-Inbox** — everything goes here first
- **40-Slipbox** — atomic notes, literature notes, synthesis
- **50-Projects** — active work with deadlines
- **60-Outputs** — finished things (articles, episodes, etc.)
- **70-Templates** — reusable templates for new notes

## Capture First, Organize Later

The most important rule: capture without friction. Every idea goes into the inbox. Organizing happens on a separate pass.

This connects to [[digital-gardening-philosophy]] and my broader approach to writing as thinking.

## Daily Notes

I maintain a daily note that tracks:
- What I'm working on
- Things I captured that need processing
- Connections I noticed

## The Key Insight

Your PKM system is only as good as the review habit attached to it. Weekly reviews are non-negotiable.
