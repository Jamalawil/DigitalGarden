# Notes on the Eremocene

E.O. Wilson coined "Eremocene" — the Age of Loneliness — to describe the geological era we are entering in which humans have so thoroughly dominated the biosphere that other species find themselves isolated in ever-shrinking habitat patches.

## Key Concepts

**Habitat fragmentation**: The breaking apart of continuous habitat into smaller, isolated patches. Species that survive in fragments often cannot reproduce or migrate.

**Acoustic isolation**: As documented by Bernie Krause, soundscapes have lost their density and complexity. The silence is data.

**The sixth extinction**: We are in the midst of an extinction event caused not by asteroid impact but by one species.
