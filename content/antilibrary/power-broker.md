Robert Moses and the fall of New York. The definitive study of how power operates outside democratic accountability. Essential for anyone interested in urban politics, investigative journalism, or the long arc of infrastructure decisions.

I keep not starting this because of its length. I keep meaning to because nothing else in the literature does what Caro does here.
