Macfarlane descends into the underground world — caves, mines, catacombs — and comes back with a meditation on deep time and the Anthropocene. His prose is the best current model for writing about ecological crisis without either sentimentality or despair. The book I most want to have read before I finish anything serious about the Eremocene.
