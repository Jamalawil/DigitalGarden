Lewis on the dismantling of federal weather and climate infrastructure — what was lost and what the costs will be. A short book but one that addresses the intersection of governmental capacity and ecological consequence that I am most interested in. On the list partly for its subject matter and partly as a study in how Lewis handles the short-form essay.
