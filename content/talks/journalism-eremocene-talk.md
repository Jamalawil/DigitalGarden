# Journalism in the Eremocene

Delivered at the International Journalism Festival, October 2024.

This talk argues that the sixth mass extinction is a story that conventional journalism is structurally unequipped to tell — and proposes a set of methodological adaptations that might help.

The core argument: journalism was designed for events, but ecological collapse is a process. The tools we have for covering what-happened-today are poorly suited to covering what-is-happening-always. We need longer time horizons, different source relationships, and a willingness to represent non-human subjects with something approaching narrative agency.
