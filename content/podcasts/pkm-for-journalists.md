# Knowledge Management for Journalists

An episode of the Building a Second Brain podcast, hosted by Tiago Forte.

The conversation covers how I adapted the PARA method for investigative journalism, where it works well (long-form research accumulation) and where it breaks down (exploratory research that crosses project boundaries).

We also discuss: the difference between a research database and a thinking tool; why most journalists' note-taking practices are surprisingly underdeveloped; and what the digital garden model offers over traditional CMS-based publishing.

This is one of the clearer explanations I have given of how I actually use these tools in practice, as opposed to how I think they should be used in theory.
