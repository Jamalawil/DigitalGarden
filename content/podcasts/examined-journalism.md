# The Examined Journalism Podcast

A conversation with host Priya Varma on the methodology behind long-form investigative journalism.

We talked through: how I approach source development in communities with high distrust of media; the tension between documentary accuracy and narrative coherence; and what I have learned from working on stories that took longer than expected to be verified.

The episode also covers my thinking on the Eremocene as a journalistic challenge — how you tell a story about ecological collapse in a medium designed for events.
